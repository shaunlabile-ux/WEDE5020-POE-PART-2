About Cotlands

Creating opportunities through early learning and play.

Who We Are

Cotlands is a South African non-profit organisation that focuses on improving the lives of young children through early learning and play-based programmes.

The organisation works with communities, families and other partners to provide children with opportunities to learn, develop and prepare for their future.

Our Mission

Our mission is to contribute towards a better future for children by supporting early childhood development and creating access to quality learning opportunities.

Our Vision

Our vision is a society where every child has the opportunity to develop, learn and reach their potential.

Our Target Community

Cotlands supports young children, families, caregivers, communities, volunteers, donors and organisations interested in supporting early childhood development.
